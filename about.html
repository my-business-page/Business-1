Basic Image Interior Design is a Singapore renovation contractor and interior design practice with roots going back to 2007. The studio has a deliberately personal character: understand the homeowner, understand the space, then make the decisions that matter.

The business is known for a hands-on design approach rather than relying entirely on elaborate visualisation tools. Research notes for this prospect specifically highlight detailed free-hand sketches used to explain how a proposed space can come together.

Based in Woodlands, the practice serves Singapore homeowners seeking renovation and interior design support. It is listed as an HDB-licensed renovation contractor.

Good design is not decoration layered onto a room. It is the way the room works, stores, flows and feels.
